Privacy Policy — Convert 'Em All

Last updated: July 12, 2026

Convert 'Em All ("the app") is a file converter for Android developed and published by The Yellow Apps. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

The short version

The app does not collect, store, or share any personal data. All file conversions happen entirely on your device. Your files are never uploaded to any server.

Files you convert

Files you select for conversion are processed locally on your device. The converted files are saved on your device only. The app does not transmit your files, their contents, or their metadata over the internet.

Text recognition (OCR)

The optional text-recognition feature uses Google ML Kit's on-device text recognition. Images are analyzed locally on your device and are not sent to any server. On first use, the recognition model may be downloaded from Google; this download does not include any of your data.

Data collected by the app

None. The app does not require an account, does not use analytics or advertising SDKs, and does not collect identifiers, usage data, or diagnostics. Settings such as your conversion history and preferences are stored only locally on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the app.

Purchases

The optional subscription is processed entirely by Google Play Billing. The app does not receive or store your payment details. Payments and refunds are handled by Google according to the Google Privacy Policy.

Permissions

The app requests only the permissions needed to convert files you explicitly choose (file access via the system file picker, and notifications to show conversion progress). Permissions are never used to scan or upload your files in the background.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.
